Idle Golf Demo

Short description:
An idle golf game about launching endless shots, earning cash, upgrading your range, retiring for medals, and unlocking stranger golf gear.

Demo contents:
- Play on the Big Piney course.
- Unlock and try Standard Ball, Pin Seeker, and Firework Ball.
- Unlock and try Standard Golfer, Shop Keep, and Lion Trees.
- Preview full-game balls, golfers, and courses in the shop.

Controls:
- Shots are automatic.
- Drag the launch control to shape shots.
- Use the aim slider to steer shots.
- Spend cash on upgrades and medals on shop unlocks.
- Use settings for window mode, resolution, VSync, SFX, music, reset, and exit.
